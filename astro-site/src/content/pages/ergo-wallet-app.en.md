## Your keys, on your phone

The Ergo Wallet app is a self-custody wallet for ERG and Ergo-based assets. Your
private keys are generated on your device and never leave it. The app has no account,
no password reset and no support desk that can recover a lost seed phrase.

## What it does

- Hold and send ERG.
- Manage tokens issued on Ergo, including NFTs.
- Connect to dApps without pasting private keys into a website.
- Watch balance and transaction history.

## Getting started

1. Install the app from the official repository or app store listing linked from the
   Ergo website.
2. Create a wallet and write the recovery phrase down, offline.
3. Fund it with ERG from an exchange listed on the Get Ergo page.

## Before you fund it

Read the recovery phrase back from your own device and confirm it restores the same
wallet on a second device. This is the only backup that matters, and it is the step
people skip.
